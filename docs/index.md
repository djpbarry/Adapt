# ADAPT

ADAPT (**A**utomated **D**etection and **A**nalysis of **P**ro**T**rusions) is an
[ImageJ/Fiji](https://fiji.sc/) plugin for automated detection and analysis of
cell migration, membrane protrusions, and associated fluorescence intensity.

It consumes two image stacks per cell — a cytoplasmic channel used for
segmentation and a "signal" channel — and produces curvature/velocity/signal
maps, per-cell and per-protrusion metrics, and visualisations.
